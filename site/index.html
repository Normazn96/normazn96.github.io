About

Nan Zhao · she/her

Counseling Psychology · Iowa State University · Expected 2028

I am a PhD candidate in Counseling Psychology at Iowa State University. My research examines self-compassion, mental health stigma and help-seeking, and well-being across cultural contexts, especially among East Asian heritage populations. My supervised clinical training includes individual and group therapy and psychological assessment.

03

Research

I study how culture and relationships shape self-compassion, distress, stigma, and help-seeking. My current work develops a culturally grounded measure of dialectical self-compassion for East Asian heritage populations using cross-cultural, psychometric, longitudinal, and daily-life methods.

Areas of focus

  • Self-compassion, self-criticism, shame, and guilt
  • Culture, dialecticism, and psychological well-being
  • Mental health stigma and help-seeking
  • Cross-cultural measurement and psychometrics
  • Social media, interpersonal contexts, and mental health
04

Clinical Training Interests

My clinical training interests center on culturally responsive care for college students and community adults. I am especially interested in self-compassion, trauma, anxiety, identity and acculturation, neurodivergence, and relationship concerns.

Areas of interest

  • Self-compassion, shame, self-criticism, and perfectionism
  • Trauma, anxiety, and related concerns
  • Identity, acculturation, and interpersonal relationships
  • Neurodivergence-affirming care
  • Group therapy and collaborative psychological assessment
